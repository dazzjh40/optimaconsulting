Training and Capability

Flexible training for officers, senior leaders and elected Members.

Optima Consulting provides environmental enforcement training for councils that want to strengthen officer confidence, improve investigation quality and create a more joined-up response to environmental crime.

Training is delivered by experienced former environmental protection officers and former police officers with backgrounds in law, enforcement and environmental crime. Sessions can be shaped around your team's workload, local priorities and the level of support required.

These topics are explained in operational terms, balancing privacy, proportionality, public protection and evidential value.

Abandoned Vehicles

Reduce officer time, remove delay and regain control of the process.

Many authorities still manage abandoned vehicles through slow, multi-step workflows that increase site visits, delay action and limit financial recovery.

Typical Challenges

  • Multiple visits for a single case
  • Delays obtaining keeper details
  • Unclear classification and ownership decisions
  • Limited value recovery from contractor-led models

Process Improvements

  • Use of DVLA devolved powers where appropriate
  • Single-visit handling in more scenarios
  • Clearer enforcement decision points
  • Better contract and cost-control arrangements
