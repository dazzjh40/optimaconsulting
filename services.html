Abandoned Vehicles

Reduce officer time, remove delay and regain control of the process.

Many authorities still manage abandoned vehicles through slow, multi-step workflows that increase site visits, delay action and limit financial recovery.

Typical Challenges

  • Multiple visits for a single case
  • Delays obtaining keeper details
  • Unclear classification and ownership decisions
  • Limited value recovery from contractor-led models

Process Improvements

  • Use of DVLA devolved powers where appropriate
  • Single-visit handling in more scenarios
  • Clearer enforcement decision points
  • Better contract and cost-control arrangements

Impact

Support focused on measurable operational improvement.

The work is designed to help teams make practical progress, whether the immediate need is backlog reduction, better case progression, training or a redesigned workflow.

  • Reduced processing time per case
  • Fewer avoidable site visits
  • Improved investigation quality
  • Stronger enforcement consistency
  • Greater confidence in public-facing response
