Abandoned Vehicles

Turn a slow statutory process into a controlled, value-aware service.

Abandoned vehicle work often sits between waste, environmental health, streetscene, parking and contractor arrangements. When ownership is unclear, processes can become slow, repetitive and costly.

Typical Service Pressure

  • Repeated visits to inspect, photograph, sticker and re-check vehicles
  • Delays tracing keepers or confirming vehicle status
  • Legacy processes built around outdated access routes and paper reports
  • Contractor-led removal models where the council carries cost but loses value

Improvement Opportunity

  • Clearer triage between abandoned, nuisance and untaxed vehicles
  • More efficient evidence, notice and decision workflows
  • Better use of lawful tools, including DVLA partnership options where suitable
  • Contract review, value recovery and stronger commercial control

Vehicle Service Redesign

Experience from leading a rural environmental protection team.

This support is grounded in recent local authority experience. Before retiring from council service, Optima's founder led an environmental protection team in a rural authority and reviewed how abandoned and untaxed vehicle reports were being handled.

Officers were spending significant time on repeat visits, warning notices, status checks, keeper tracing and contractor-led removals. At the same time, older DVLA checking routes were being withdrawn, pushing many councils toward slower paper-based processes.

The review identified a wider opportunity: not just to remove vehicles, but to redesign the process, improve governance, revisit contract terms, recognise vehicles with recoverable value and consider DVLA Devolved Power Partnership arrangements where appropriate.

Proven Direction of Travel

After the revised approach was introduced, vehicle sales and fee recovery generated significant income, helping ensure the council's operational costs were well covered.

The website does not set out the full model, because the value is in understanding the legal, operational, governance and commercial risks before changing the process.
